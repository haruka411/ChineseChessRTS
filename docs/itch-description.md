# Chinese Chess RTS / 即时象棋 RTS

## English

Chinese Chess RTS turns Xiangqi into a real-time strategy duel. Capture mines, spend income to build new pieces, and pressure the enemy palace before your king is hunted down. Pieces still follow familiar Xiangqi movement, but cooldowns, economy, and hidden AI strategies make every match unstable and fast-moving.

Play defensively for mines, raid the opponent's back rank to shut down powerful builds, or rush the king before the economy matters.

## 中文

《即时象棋 RTS》把中国象棋改造成一场即时战略对局。你需要占领矿点、用收入建造新棋子，并在己方将帅被猎杀前压迫对方九宫。棋子的走法仍接近传统象棋，但冷却、经济和隐藏 AI 策略会让局势不断变化。

你可以稳守矿点，也可以突袭对方底线压制车马建造，或者在经济成型前直接冲击将帅。
